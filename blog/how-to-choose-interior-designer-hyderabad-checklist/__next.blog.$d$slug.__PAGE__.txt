8:T7b1,<h2 id="selecting-a-trusted-design-partner-in-hyderabad">Selecting a Trusted Design Partner in Hyderabad</h2>
<p>With hundreds of interior firms and local contractors operating across Hyderabad, finding a reliable design partner who delivers quality on time can be challenging. Many homeowners encounter delayed timelines, low-grade material substitutions, or unexpected cost escalations halfway through the project.</p>
<p>Use this 10-point checklist before signing an interior agreement in Hyderabad:</p>
<p>---</p>
<h3 id="1-do-they-own-or-operate-a-local-manufacturing-facility">1. Do They Own or Operate a Local Manufacturing Facility?</h3>
<p>Firms that outsource woodwork to unverified third-party carpenters frequently face quality inconsistencies and delays. <strong>DV Homes operates its own production facility in Kokapet</strong>, allowing direct quality control over cutting, edge-banding, and finishing.</p>
<h3 id="2-are-material-specifications-explicitly-written-in-the-contract">2. Are Material Specifications Explicitly Written in the Contract?</h3>
<p>Ensure the BOQ lists exact brand names, sheet thickness (e.g., 18mm BWP marine plywood for carcass, 8mm for backings), and laminate codes.</p>
<h3 id="3-can-you-visit-an-active-project-site">3. Can You Visit an Active Project Site?</h3>
<p>Reputable designers should be confident in arranging live site walkthroughs in areas like <strong>Gachibowli, Madhapur, or Kokapet</strong> so you can inspect ongoing work quality.</p>
<h3 id="4-what-hardware-brand-amp-warranty-is-offered">4. What Hardware Brand &amp; Warranty Is Offered?</h3>
<p>Insist on branded soft-close hinges and drawer slides (Blum, Hafele, Hettich) backed by a minimum <strong>10-Year Warranty</strong>.</p>
<h3 id="5-is-pricing-locked-or-subject-to-escalation">5. Is Pricing Locked or Subject to Escalation?</h3>
