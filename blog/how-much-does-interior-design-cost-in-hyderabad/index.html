Understanding Home Interior Costs in Hyderabad (2026)

Planning the interior design for a new apartment or villa in Hyderabad is one of the most exciting milestones for any homeowner. However, navigating interior quotes can often feel overwhelming when different contractors and design firms present estimates ranging anywhere from ₹5 Lakhs to ₹30+ Lakhs for what seems like similar floorplans.

To help you budget with confidence, this guide breaks down real interior design costs across popular Hyderabad neighborhoods—such as Madhapur, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Kokapet, Financial District, Jubilee Hills, and Kompally.

Component-Wise Cost Breakdown

To understand where your money goes, let's analyze the core cost components of a complete 3BHK interior project in Hyderabad:

1. Modular Kitchen & Pantry (20% - 25% of Budget)

  • Essential (Laminate + Commercial Plywood): ₹1.5L - ₹2.2L
  • Premium (BWP Marine Plywood + Anti-Fingerprint Acrylic + Quartz): ₹2.5L - ₹4.2L
  • Luxury (BWP Plywood + PU Lacquer + Calacatta Quartz + Hafele Accessories): ₹4.5L - ₹8.0L

2. Wardrobes & Bedroom Storage (30% - 35% of Budget)

  • Hinged Laminate Wardrobes (per sq.ft. of elevation): ₹1,200 - ₹1,600 / sq.ft.
  • Sliding Acrylic Wardrobes with Loft: ₹1,800 - ₹2,400 / sq.ft.
  • Fluted Glass & PVD Frame Walk-in Closet: ₹2,600 - ₹3,800 / sq.ft.

Key Factors That Impact Your Final Quote

1. Substrate Material (Plywood Grade)

Never compromise on plywood in Hyderabad's climate. Commercial MR (Moisture Resistant) plywood is suitable for dry bedroom wardrobes, but 100% BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Marine Plywood (IS 710 certified) is mandatory for kitchens, bathrooms, and utility zones to prevent water damage and termite infestation.

2. Shutter Finishes

  • Laminate (1mm): Budget-friendly, highly durable, available in matt and gloss.
  • Acrylic (2mm): High-gloss glass-like reflection, scratch-resistant, anti-fingerprint.
  • Natural Veneer: Authentic wood grain warmth, requires PU clear polish coating.
  • PU Lacquer Paint: Seamless luxury finish with zero edge lines.

3. Hardware Brands

Using genuine German fittings from Blum, Hafele, or Hettich adds 15-20% to hardware cost but guarantees smooth soft-close drawer operation for 10-15 years without sagging.

---

How to Avoid Hidden Costs

1. Demand an Itemized BOQ: Ensure your quote explicitly lists plywood brand, sheet thickness, laminate code, hardware model, and ceiling material.

2. Verify Factory Edge-Banding: Hand-glued edge banding peels off within 2 years. Factory-applied PUR hotmelt edge banding provides a 100% waterproof seal.

3. Check Turnkey Scope: Confirm if electrical alterations, magnetic track lights, ceiling paint, quartz installation, and post-site deep cleaning are included.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of 3BHK interior design in Hyderabad?

A standard quality 3BHK interior in Hyderabad ranges from ₹6.5 Lakhs to ₹10.5 Lakhs for essential modular woodwork. Premium or luxury 3BHK interiors with acrylic/veneer finishes, quartz countertops, false ceiling, and lighting range between ₹12 Lakhs and ₹22 Lakhs.

How much does a modular kitchen cost in Hyderabad?

A modular kitchen built with 100% BWP Marine Plywood carcass, acrylic shutters, quartz counter, and Blum/Hafele soft-close hardware typically costs between ₹1.8 Lakhs and ₹4.5 Lakhs depending on kitchen layout size and basket accessories.

Why do interior design quotes vary so widely between contractors and studios in Hyderabad?

Price variations stem from substrate material grade (Commercial MR Plywood vs 100% BWP Marine Plywood), shutter finish (thin laminate vs 2mm acrylic vs natural veneer), hardware brand (local vs Blum/Hafele), edge-banding technology, and inclusion of turnkey services like civil/electrical work.
